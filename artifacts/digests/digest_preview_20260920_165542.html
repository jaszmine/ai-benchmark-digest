Track B Flagship Breakthrough Deep-Dive

PrismML’s Ternary Bonsai 2 27B achieves a 9.1× size reduction by converting Alibaba’s Qwen3.8 27B into a ternary (−1,0,+1) weight representation. Instead of storing full‑precision FP16 values, each weight is encoded with roughly 1.71 bits via blockwise Hadamard rotation, a preprocessing step that spreads information across orthogonal bases before quantization, which mitigates the typical accuracy loss of low‑bit schemes. The rotated blocks are then quantized to three levels, yielding an effective storage of 5.9 GB from the original 54 GB. Despite the aggressive compression, the model retains 98.2% of aggregate benchmark scores, with near‑perfect math and coding retention, indicating that the rotation preserves the most salient directions in weight space. Inference speed benefits from the compact footprint: 142.5 tokens per second on an RTX 5090 and 46.8 tok/s on an M5 Max, allowing the model to reside in 16 GB of laptop RAM. Trade‑offs include reliance on custom kernels for the Hadamard rotation and potential sensitivity to outliers that ternary quantization cannot capture, which may affect niche tasks. The Apache 2.0 license encourages community adoption, and the approach demonstrates a viable path for deploying large language models on edge devices, reducing cloud inference costs while preserving most of the model’s capability.

Results on GSM‑8K and MATH show up to 30% energy savings for equivalent accuracy.
When2Think: Learning Difficulty-Aware Length Control for Efficient Hybrid Reasoning Models
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ▲ 33 upvotes • ✓ 200 OK
Presents a length‑control module that learns to adjust the reasoning token budget of Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) based on estimated problem difficulty, mitigating overthinking on easy cases and underthinking on hard ones. The module is trained via reinforcement learning with a reward that balances correctness and generation cost. On benchmarks such as ARC‑Challenge and BigBench‑Reasoning, When2Think reduces average generation length by 22% while maintaining or improving accuracy.
