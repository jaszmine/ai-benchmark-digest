A*-Thought-V2: Efficient Latent Reasoning via Geometric Dynamics of LLM
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ✓ 200 OK
Models the chain-of-thought process as a geometric flow in the model's hidden state space, enabling efficient inference without expanding context length. Ablation studies demonstrate robustness across varying reasoning depths and task domains.

Revisiting Complete Reasoning Traces for Post-Training
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ✓ 200 OK
Examines the effect of using full‑length reasoning trajectories—rather than truncated snippets—in post‑training LLMs to boost complex reasoning. The authors propose a curriculum that gradually extends trace length to stabilize learning and prevent overfitting.
