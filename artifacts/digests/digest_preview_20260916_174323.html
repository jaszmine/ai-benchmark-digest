Track B Flagship Breakthrough Deep-Dive

Abacus.AI’s Smaug family represents a strategic shift toward massive, open‑weight foundation models that are subsequently specialized for agentic enterprise tasks. Smaug Agentic inherits the 2‑trillion‑parameter architecture of Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3, while Smaug Flash is based on DeepSeek V4 Flash; both are released with full weights, enabling community inspection and reuse. The models are then fine‑tuned on curated agent‑oriented datasets—covering tool use, planning, and feedback loops—to align behavior with enterprise workflows. This two‑stage approach decouples the costly pretraining phase from the cheaper specialization step, allowing a single large base to serve many downstream agents. Tradeoffs include substantial inference compute due to the model’s size, which is mitigated through quantization and efficient serving kernels, and the inherent safety risks of releasing powerful open weights that could be repurposed for malicious agentic use. For practitioners, Smaug lowers the barrier to deploying sophisticated AI agents by cutting claimed operational costs by two orders of magnitude, while also demanding robust governance, monitoring, and alignment practices to manage the increased autonomy and potential misuse inherent in such scalable, open models.

StepAudio 3 Realtime Technical Report
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ▲ 91 upvotes • ✓ 200 OK
StepAudio 3 Realtime is an audio-language foundation model built for low-latency spoken interaction, featuring a continuous listen-coordinate architecture that tightly couples audio encoders with language decoders. It achieves sub-200ms end-to-end latency on commodity GPUs while supporting reasoning over long contexts via chunked attention mechanisms. The model comprises 1.5B parameters, trained on 1M hours of multilingual speech and text using a hybrid transformer‑conformer backbone and a novel turn‑taking token scheme.
StepAudio 3 Music Technical Report
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ▲ 73 upvotes • ✓ 200 OK
StepAudio 3 Music is a large-scale, long-form music generation model that enables explicit musical planning (e.g., chord progressions, structure) and open-domain text-to-music control. It employs a music-specific tokenizer that encodes rhythm, pitch, and timbre into discrete tokens, allowing transformer-based generation of coherent pieces up to several minutes. The model has 2B parameters, trained on 200k hours of licensed audio, outputs at 32kHz, and supports a 128-token musical planning horizon.
