OpenWAM: An Open, Modular Exploration Towards Systematic World-Action Model Pretraining
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ▲ 10 upvotes • ✗ Status {'url': 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07398', 'status': 200, 'healthy': True, 'is_protected': False, 'error': None}
OpenWAM proposes a modular framework that leverages video-generative priors to imbue world knowledge into executable control signals for embodied agents. By decoupling perception, dynamics, and policy components, it enables systematic pretraining and transfer across tasks. The approach demonstrates improved sample efficiency and generalization in simulated robotics benchmarks.
Show-Harness: Just a VLM Agent Can Play Robots
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ▲ 7 upvotes • ✗ Status {'url': 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10522', 'status': 200, 'healthy': True, 'is_protected': False, 'error': None}
Show-Harness adapts foundation vision-language models to generate robot actions directly from language instructions via an embodied harness that aligns VLM outputs with low-level motor commands. The method bypasses traditional policy learning by prompting the VLM with task descriptions and retrieving corresponding action sequences from a pre-collected corpus. Experiments show competitive performance on manipulation and navigation tasks without fine-tuning.
Φ-Bench: Can Large Language Models Engineer the Infrastructure That Powers Them?

Φ-Bench introduces a benchmark suite evaluating LLMs' ability to generate, optimize, and validate infrastructure-as-code scripts for cloud services, networking, and security policies. It measures correctness, security compliance, and performance across tasks such as Terraform generation and Kubernetes configuration. Results reveal a significant gap between code generation proficiency and operational reliability, highlighting needed improvements in tool-aware reasoning.
Programmable World Model
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ▲ 5 upvotes • ✗ Status {'url': 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10540', 'status': 200, 'healthy': True, 'is_protected': False, 'error': None}
This work extends video world models with explicit mechanisms for maintaining persistent world state and enforcing user-defined rules through a differentiable programming interface. By integrating a neural scene graph with a rule engine, the model can simulate long-horizon interactions while guaranteeing constraint satisfaction. Evaluations show improved fidelity in interactive environments and the ability to compose complex behaviors via logical specifications.
Diffs vs. Whole Files: An Empirical Comparison of Iterative Edit-Based and Direct Generation for Flutter/Dart Code Models
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ▲ 5 upvotes • ✗ Status {'url': 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05779', 'status': 200, 'healthy': True, 'is_protected': False, 'error': None}
The paper compares two paradigms for training code LLMs: direct generation of entire modified files versus iterative diff-based edits. Using a Flutter/Dart benchmark, it finds that diff-based models achieve higher edit accuracy and lower syntax error rates, especially for large-scale refactorings, while direct generation excels at small, localized changes. Ablation studies reveal the impact of tokenization and loss weighting on edit quality.
