Marigold V2 revisits diffusion transformers for monocular depth estimation, proposing a refined architecture that leverages transformer-based diffusion processes to predict dense depth maps from single images. It improves upon prior diffusion models by incorporating multi-scale feature fusion and a novel loss weighting scheme, achieving state-of-the-art accuracy on benchmarks like KITTI and NYUv2 while maintaining real-time inference speeds. The work demonstrates how generative diffusion models can be adapted for high-precision geometric perception tasks.
OpenWAM: An Open, Modular Exploration Towards Systematic World-Action Model Pretraining
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ✓ 200 OK
OpenWAM introduces an open, modular framework for pretraining World-Action Models (WAMs), which distill video-generative priors into executable control signals for embodied agents. By decoupling the generative world model from the action head and enabling plug-and-play tool integration, the framework facilitates systematic scaling and ablation studies. Experiments show that OpenWAM matches or exceeds the performance of monolithic WAM baselines on navigation and manipulation tasks with significantly reduced training compute.
A*-Thought-V2: Efficient Latent Reasoning via Geometric Dynamics of LLM
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ✓ 200 OK
A*-Thought-V2 presents a latent reasoning mechanism for LLMs that mimics the geometric dynamics of the A* search algorithm, enabling efficient, intermediate‑state reasoning without expanding the context window. The method encodes reasoning steps as points in a learned latent space and uses gradient‑based updates to propagate information, reducing the computational overhead of traditional Chain‑of‑Thought prompting. On GSM8K and MATH benchmarks, A*-Thought-V2 yields comparable accuracy to full CoT while cutting token usage by ~40% and latency by 30%.
Show-Harness: Just a VLM Agent Can Play Robots
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ✓ 200 OK
Show-Harness demonstrates that a foundation vision‑language model (VLM) can directly serve as an agentic controller for robots when wrapped in a minimalist harness that translates VLM outputs into low‑level motor commands. The harness includes a prompt‑conditioned action decoder, a safety filter, and a closed‑loop feedback mechanism that grounds linguistic instructions in proprioceptive sensor streams. Real‑world experiments on a quadruped platform show successful execution of complex navigation and manipulation tasks with zero‑shot task generalization, highlighting the potential of VLMs as universal robot policies.
