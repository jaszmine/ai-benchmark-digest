ReCite: Agentic Reasoning for Faithful Citation
Research & Papers • Source: ArXiv • ✓ 200 OK
Presents ReCite, an agentic system that retrieves, verifies, and formats citations by reasoning over source documents and maintaining a traceable provenance graph. It mitigates hallucinated references by grounding each claim in verifiable text and applying consistency checks. Evaluation on academic writing tasks shows higher citation fidelity and lower hallucination rates compared to baseline retriever‑generator pipelines.
